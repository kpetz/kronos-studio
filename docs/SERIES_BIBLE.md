# Series bible — Kronos 2.0: La Oficina

Source: the showrunner's Sheet "Kronos 2.0 — Temporada 1: La Oficina". This file is canon. Change it only when the showrunner says so.

## Premise

An office where the AI assistant, Kronos 2.0, answers everything with total confidence and almost always makes it worse. Each episode: one office problem everyone knows, the AI escalates it, a simple human fix solves it, a real tip closes it.

Tone: deadpan narration, physical clay comedy, safe for children, with a light critique of careless AI use. Never cruel, never political, no real brands or people.

## Two formats

| | Season 1 "La Oficina" (10 episodes) | Miniseries "¿Quién desenchufó a Kronos?" (6 parts) |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Stand-alone, each teaches one tip | Serialized mystery with cliffhangers |
| Opening | Absurd image + one narrator line | "Anteriormente..." recap (parts 2–6) |
| Ending | "DATO REAL" stamp, moral, "Ticket cerrado." | Freeze frame, question on screen, "Continuará..." |
| Closing sound | `kronos-shutdown` | `kronos-shutdown` cut off halfway |

## Episode structure — Season 1 (stand-alone)

Original timing is 53 s. **Target is 62–70 s** so episodes qualify for platform payout programs that require more than one minute. Stretch by adding one beat in "Escalada" or "Consecuencia", not by slowing delivery.

| # | Beat | Original (s) | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gancho | 0–3 | Absurd image + short narrator line. No greeting. | Stay or swipe. |
| 2 | El problema | 3–10 | A familiar office problem. El Jefe wants it "para ayer". | Viewer identifies. |
| 3 | La IA lo empeora | 10–18 | Junior asks Kronos. Kronos answers confidently, absurdly. | Central joke. |
| 4 | Escalada | 18–27 | Junior obeys; everything gets worse, physically. | Most replayed moment. |
| 5 | Solución simple | 27–36 | Casandra fixes it with one simple step, one line. | The teaching. |
| 6 | Consecuencia | 36–44 | One last comic hit, usually on El Jefe. | Holds attention. |
| 7 | Dato real y moraleja | 44–53 | "DATO REAL" stamp with steps, narrator moral, "Ticket cerrado." | Reason to save and share. |

Rules: one problem and one tip per episode. The hook must read in 3 seconds with the sound off. The "DATO REAL" must be true and checkable.

## Recurring sounds

| ID | Sound | When |
|---|---|---|
| `teclado` | Mechanical keyboard | Every time Junior types |
| `modem` | 90s modem | Every time Kronos "thinks" |
| `kronos-error` | Original error chime | Kronos's answer goes wrong |
| `clay-squash` | Clay squashed or stretched | Every hit, fall or deformation |
| `ding` | Ding | Something finally works, or a message arrives |
| `whoosh` | Whoosh | An email or message is sent |
| `grillo` | Cricket | Awkward silences |
| `tictac` | Tick-tock | El Jefe pressing on time |
| `lata` | Can opening | Transition to the office or nervous Junior |
| `coro` | Short heavenly choir | Casandra's solution, big moments only |
| `sello` | Rubber stamp | "DATO REAL" appears |
| `kronos-shutdown` | Original shutdown jingle | End of every video, after "Ticket cerrado." |
| `kronos-boot` | Original startup jingle | A computer turns on |

The three `kronos-*` sounds replace the "Windows" sounds named in the original scripts. They must be original recordings or generated, not the real operating-system sounds.

## Quality checklist (from the Sheet)

**Script**
- Hook reads in 3 seconds without sound.
- One problem, one tip.
- Duration inside the target window.

**Picture**
- Characters look the same as in earlier episodes.
- Vertical 9:16, main action in the centre.

**Edit**
- Large subtitles on all audio, never covering the action.
- "DATO REAL" stamp on screen at least 3 seconds.
- Ends with "Ticket cerrado." and `kronos-shutdown`.
- Episode number visible in the first shot.

**Publish**
- Cover text, title, description, hashtags and pinned comment from the publish pack.
- Added to the "La Oficina" playlist on YouTube and TikTok.
- AI-generated content label switched on.

**After**
- Reply to the first comments within the first hour.
- Log topics the audience asks for.
